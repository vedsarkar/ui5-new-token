Endorse 12 SAP Fiori primitives from `@reltio/design/components` as 1:1 re-exports (doc-only directories with README, prop-type schema, and variant stories): `FlexBox`, `Grid`, `Card`, `CardHeader`, `ExpandableText`, `RatingIndicator`, `NumericSideIndicator`, `ToggleButton`, `ColorPicker`, `Page`, `Carousel`, and `DynamicSideContent`. These cover common layout containers, card surfaces, and small display/input controls so apps no longer reach for them via direct UI5 imports.
