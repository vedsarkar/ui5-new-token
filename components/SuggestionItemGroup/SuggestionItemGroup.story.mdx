# SuggestionItemGroup


`SuggestionItemGroup` groups `Input` suggestions under a header.

`SuggestionItemGroup` is re-exported 1:1 from `@ui5/webcomponents-react/SuggestionItemGroup`; its props, slots, and behavior are exactly the UI5 component's. The Reltio layer adds curation (the endorsed surface), pinned versioning, and documentation.

### Usage
