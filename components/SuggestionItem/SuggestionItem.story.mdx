
`SuggestionItem` is re-exported 1:1 from `@ui5/webcomponents-react/SuggestionItem`; its props, slots, and behavior are exactly the UI5 component's. The Reltio layer adds curation (the endorsed surface), pinned versioning, and documentation.
