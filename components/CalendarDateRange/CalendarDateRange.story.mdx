# CalendarDateRange


`CalendarDateRange` marks a selected start/end range inside a range-mode `Calendar`.

`CalendarDateRange` is re-exported 1:1 from `@ui5/webcomponents-react/CalendarDateRange`; its props, slots, and behavior are exactly the UI5 component's. The Reltio layer adds curation (the endorsed surface), pinned versioning, and documentation.

### Usage

Use with `Calendar` `selectionMode="Range"`; set `startValue`/`endValue`.
